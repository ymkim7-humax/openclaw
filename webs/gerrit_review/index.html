[ARHT-5][UXD] Home - Attached Devices : Fix incorrect connected devices
LGTM
Project: airoha/2025q3 | Owner: Deokwon Kang(강덕원)
The patch correctly implements the requirement to avoid deleting bridge FDB entries when a port is down on platforms with `HNI_SUPPORT_COMMON` defined. 

- **Logic**: By adding a `continue` before the `fdb_delete` call within the port-down check, it ensures that the FDB entry persists even if the physical link is temporarily lost. This is consistent with the goal of fixing "incorrect connected devices" issues where devices might disappear and reappear in the UI due to FDB entry cycling.
- **Scope**: The use of `HNI_SUPPORT_COMMON` correctly limits the impact to the relevant Humax-specified platforms (like HP2272B) without affecting other product variants.
- **Safety**: The `continue` statement is safe within the `hlist_for_each_entry_safe` loop used in `host_list_read_proc`.
- **Style**: The implementation follows the existing coding style and macro usage patterns found in `ecnt_br.h`.
